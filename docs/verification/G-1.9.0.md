It changes no document schema: the highest schema stays 35, and the downgrade window is 1.8.0-1, which opens every file 1.9.0 writes within the 1.8.0 limits and refuses, with its own message and no change to the file, one over them. The procedure is [M10 acceptance](../M10_ACCEPTANCE.md), "The 1.9.0 release". The form of the record is the [1.8.0 record](G-1.8.0.md). The prior record is G-1.8.0.

## What 1.9.0 contains

From [the release notes](../RELEASE_NOTES.md#190):

- **Raised model limits (R115, measured in R114).** Every model limit is about eight times 1.8.0's, or set by its measurement: unique and materialized vertices, faces, wires and edges, bodies and placements; 4,096 component definitions per document; a native file of 2 GiB; a model chunk of 768 MiB; a read-snapshot budget of 4 GiB, one budget for every export, render and library insert; an undo budget of 4 GiB; a GLB visible-face cap of 4,800,000. The full table is in [the release notes](../RELEASE_NOTES.md#limits). No document schema, file format version or error code changes. The owner said on 2026-11-07 that the earlier 3.75 and 8 GiB memory ceilings protected only the development laptop, and that no product limit is derived from them ([R082w v4](R082w-memory-budget-v4.md) records the earlier ceilings).
- **R113, the file formats take the document's limits (D-102, [#582](https://github.com/zygote55/sketchyup/pull/582)).** OBJ, STL, glTF, DXF and `.skp` follow the document limits. A mesh over the one-body limit imports as several bodies. STEP is not in this fix (D-103).
- **R117, the edit path (D-104, D-106, D-107, [#584](https://github.com/zygote55/sketchyup/pull/584)).** A move and a definition edit validate only what they change. The undo charge counts each changed record once. Push and pull work on any body inside the one-body limit, and the 1,000-face context limit is gone. The 4,096-definition count is one limit for the saved file, the loader, the glTF import and the `.skp` import.
- **R118, viewport textures (D-105, [#586](https://github.com/zygote55/sketchyup/pull/586)).** The viewport holds 1 GiB of decoded textures and 1,024 images. When the textures at full size exceed that, the largest are shown at reduced resolution. A raster export of such a view is refused (D-110).
- **R119, glTF meshes used by one node (D-108, [#587](https://github.com/zygote55/sketchyup/pull/587)).** A mesh used by one node imports as the bodies of its node's group. A mesh used by several nodes is one component definition, as before.
- **Two fixes found by the preparation.** D-111 (the capability output reported `componentDefinitions` as 1,024; it reads 4,096) and D-112 (the Blender worker's GLB bound was R113's 1,800,000 triangles; it is now the export's bound). Neither was refused or written wrongly before its fix; both are fixed in the preparation ([#588](https://github.com/zygote55/sketchyup/pull/588)).
- **The limits correction ([#581](https://github.com/zygote55/sketchyup/pull/581)).** The documentation no longer describes the 8 GiB ceiling as a product limit.
- **Documentation.** The release notes, the support matrix, the user guide, the changelog, the roadmap rows R113 to R119 and ADR 0162's notes.
- **Schema.** None. 1.9.0 reads document versions 1 through 35 and writes 35 at most. The downgrade window is 1.8.0-1, with the refusals in [Downgrade window and rollback](#downgrade-window-and-rollback).

## Accepted misses and limits

The M10 row "Accepted misses" for 1.9.0 lists D-099, D-100, D-101, D-103, D-107, D-109, D-110 and D-087 as the limits that stay open, and D-091 for the other hardware. The waived GPU PC row is a separate item, below. They are:

- **D-099, imported UVs.** A model with imported UVs reaches the 768 MiB model chunk at about 1.2 million textured faces (1.8.0: about 136,000). The one-record storage change was left out by the owner (see [Open for the owner](#open-for-the-owner)).
- **D-100, colours and textures at the budget.** The read-snapshot budget is 4 GiB now. A coloured or textured model at the materialized limit has not been exported under it. The row stays open.
- **D-101, a sanitized transaction deadline.** In a sanitized build, `unique_limits_acceptance` failed once with `TRANSACTION_EXPIRED` at 1.8.0 (the 60 s transaction default). The row stays open. The 1.9.0 limit program was not run sanitized at the RC.
- **D-103, STEP export.** The STEP bound is 512 MiB, not measured. At 256 MiB of output a STEP export of distinct geometry stopped at about 240,000 triangles. A 512 MiB run crashed the helper after 90 s, with its container at the 4 GiB cap, and the cause is not confirmed. The STEP case did not run on the GPU PC.
- **D-107, creating definitions.** Creating many component definitions is still superlinear: 1,024 take 6 to 8 s on the development laptop, and 4,096 take 223 s there (118 s on the GPU PC). Edits are faster after R117.
- **D-109, large edits.** At the R115 limits, large edits take seconds to minutes (a definition edit at the materialized limit 100 s, a move of an 800,000-face body 10.9 s, the open of a materialized model 42.5 s, and the OBJ and STL imports of 2,400,000 faces 112 s and 189 s). Nothing is refused or lost. The owner chose to slow down rather than refuse at these sizes (2026-11-07).
- **D-110, raster exports of reduced textures.** A view that shows any texture at reduced resolution cannot be exported as a PNG, drawing view, measured drawing or thumbnail. Format exports read the stored textures and are not affected.
- **D-087, retained renders.** A retained render can carry a terrain's source notice outside its view. Minor, open.
- **D-091, hardware evidence.** The AMD GPU is closed by R114. The other hardware is open, and the Intel campaigns stay waived by [ADR 0161](../decisions/0161-m9-hardware-evidence-waiver.md).

The waived row, "At-limit acceptance (GPU PC, through the RC build)", is answered in [the table above](#answers-to-m10s-190-table). It was waived on 2026-11-10 and is not an accepted miss.
