1.6.1 is a patch of 1.6.0. It carries two fixes to the durable outcome store, which MCP and assistant commits use:
D-089 (Major: the budget counted the in-memory model, not the stored container) and D-086 (Limitation: the budget was
64 MiB, too small for a container over about 32 MiB). It has no feature, format or schema change. The highest schema
stays 35, and 1.6.1 reads and writes what 1.6.0 does. The downgrade window is 1.6.0-1.

## Commits

The outcome budget is now 268,812,472 bytes: 2 × 126,017,628 bytes (the largest container a document can have) plus
16 MiB. A before and an after copy of the largest container that can be saved fit in it.
